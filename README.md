## 🙋‍♂️ About Me
I am an M.S. student in **Applied Artificial Intelligence** at Kwangwoon University, advised by **Prof. Minsu Cho**. I completed my B.S. in **Information Convergence (Data Science)** at the same university in Feb. 2026.

My research centers on **label-efficient learning** — how models can generalize from scarce supervision — with applications in **remote sensing time-series**, **process mining**, and **NLP**. I am also interested in when modern sequence architectures (attention, Transformers) actually pay off outside the large-data regime they were designed for.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Few-shot learning for satellite time-series (IUCN ecosystem classification), Federated learning for Predictive Process Monitoring, Dual-process modeling of online reviews.
- 🎓 **Education:** M.S. in Applied AI (Mar. 2026 ~ ) / B.S. in Information Convergence (Feb. 2026), Major GPA: **4.31/4.5**.
- 💼 **Experience:** Research Intern → Graduate Researcher at PADA Lab (Process Automation & Data Analytics).

## 💻 Key Projects

### 🛰️ Few-Shot Learning for Ecosystem Classification
> *Graduate Research (2026 ~ )*
- **Goal:** Classify IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology (GET) classes from satellite time-series with only a handful of labeled samples per class.
- **Data:** HLS (Harmonized Landsat–Sentinel-2) time-series over South Korea — 24 GET classes across 6 ecological realms.
- **Approach:** ProtoNet variants over InceptionTime / Presto encoders, hierarchical margin losses, and sibling-repulsion regularization for fine-grained, same-realm class separation.

### 🏥 Federated Learning for Predictive Process Monitoring
> *Graduate Research (2026)*
- **Goal:** Predict ED-to-inpatient admission from emergency department event logs without centralizing patient data.
- **Data:** MIMICEL v2.1.0 (MIMIC-IV ED event logs, ~425K cases).
- **Findings:** Compared FedAvg / FedProx / Ditto / SCAFFOLD under realistic label-skew across sites; FedProx's advantage grows monotonically with heterogeneity.

### 🍎 Lec-Q: AI-based Interactive Learning Platform
> *Project Lead & Main Developer (Sep 2025 ~ Dec 2025)*
- **Goal:** Resolve communication gaps in online lectures.
- **Features:** Real-time handwriting sharing (WebRTC), RAG-based AI Q&A Chatbot, student concentration analytics dashboard.
- **Stack:** React, Node.js, WebRTC, OpenAI API (RAG).

### 🕵️ Dark Pattern Detection Service
> *Full Stack Developer & AI Researcher (Jan 2025 ~ Sep 2025)*
- **Goal:** Detect deceptive designs (Dark Patterns) on websites and kiosks.
- **Tech:** Neuro-Symbolic AI (BERT + logical reasoning) for high precision.
- **Output:** Chrome Extension & Web Dashboard.
